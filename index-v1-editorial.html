    Inclusive design

    Built for every ability

    Inclusivity is not an afterthought here. Barrier-free design lets residents of all abilities move freely, independently and with dignity.

    • Wheelchair access rampsInclined planes alongside stairs in all common areas.
    • Wheelchair-friendly parkingWider spots closest to entrances, with 5 ft access aisles.
    • Wide, accessible elevators900–1100 mm doors; cabins at least 1 m × 1.25 m.
    • Braille & voice-enabled liftsTactile buttons and audio feedback.
    • Accessible toiletsWide-door common restrooms for mobility aids.
    • Easy-movement doorsWider openings, minimal thresholds, accessible hardware.

    Sustainability

    Building for the bigger purpose

    IGBC-aligned practices cut environmental impact while making everyday life more comfortable: 20–30% energy savings and 30–50% water savings from day one.

    • Rainwater harvestingCaptures and reuses rainwater, recharging groundwater.
    • Solar powerPowers common areas, reducing energy use.
    • Waste managementRecycles water and turns organic waste into manure.
    • EV charging pointsReady for the shift to electric vehicles.
    • Energy-efficient lightingLEDs that use up to 30% less energy.
    • Natural light & airLarge windows for daylight and fresh air.
